The Temple of Atal'Hakkar

Sunken Temple · go at 50–54 (dungeon range 49–56) · Swamp of Sorrows · entrance

Everything a Horde paladin and warrior need before they zone in: the five quest chains that unlock the temple's quests, what to carry, the order of things inside, and where to turn it all in. Most of the unlocking happens on the way through Feralas, Tanaris, Zul'Farrak, the Hinterlands and Un'Goro, so start early: the first pickups open at level 38.
