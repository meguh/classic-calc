The top 10 items per slot for a level 60 Fury (dual wield) warrior, using only gear that is in WoW Forever now: dungeons, quests, crafting, reputation, vendors and world drops, plus PvP gear (honor ranks and battleground factions) if you switch it on. No raid loot. Two-handers use the Arms weights. Click ✓ on items you already have (two for rings and trinkets). The lists then re-rank against your gear. Hit is worth full value up to 8%, about a third from 8% to 27% when dual wielding, and nothing past 8% for the two-hander. Expertise is worth nothing past 6.5%. An item that takes you over a cap only shows as an upgrade if its other stats still win. Scores use the wowsims Forever preset stat weights and are in attack power (EP). Stamina, armor and set bonuses count as zero. Procs that don't grant stats (extra attacks, damage, stuns) are flagged in orange and not scored.
