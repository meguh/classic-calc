The top 5 items per slot for a level 60 Fury (dual wield) warrior. Two-handers use the Arms weights. Every leather, mail and plate piece, plus cloaks, jewellery, trinkets, weapons and ranged items a warrior can use, is scored with the wowsims Forever preset stat weights. The score (EP) is shown in attack power. Stamina and armor count as zero. Set bonuses and hit cap aren't included, so stop stacking hit at 8%. Procs that grant stats are averaged in. Procs that don't (extra attacks, damage, stuns) are flagged in orange and not scored, so judge those yourself.
